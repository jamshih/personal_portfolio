01 / WrongBook

WrongBook

An AI-native iOS study workflow for turning missed questions into structured review. Students scan a problem, extract the question and diagram, record why they missed it, and revisit it later instead of losing it in a photo album.

  • Built the native iOS app, backend, AI scan pipeline, subscriptions, and account flows.
  • Worked on OCR + diagram extraction, structured math rendering, sync, reliability, and privacy boundaries.
  • Swift · Vision · Supabase · StoreKit · Edge Functions

02 / SearchRecall

SearchRecall

A personal search engine for browser history. I built it because normal history search becomes nearly useless once you have years of browsing data and only remember what a page was about—not its exact title or URL.

  • Designed around large Chrome History / Takeout imports, including 100k+ records.
  • Combined AI-assisted retrieval with local fallbacks so useful recall does not depend on one model call.
  • Web app · search / retrieval · cloud backend · AI
